My Experiences as an ICT Student

Being an ICT student isn't easy, but it has been a challenging and fun experience for me. At first, I thought ICT was all about computers and technology. However, I discovered that it also involves understanding how computers work, how they behave, and how they should function. I also used to wonder whether I could understand all the lessons and learn coding. But as I continued studying, I realized that ICT is actually fun and interesting. Although I have experienced many difficulties, I still manage to accomplish the tasks related to our lessons. Through these experiences, I am learning to be more patient, persistent, and willing to improve my skills.
